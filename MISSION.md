# OPTIMA Mission

## Why This Exists
To build a disciplined, auditable trading system that helps Matthew safely 
progress from paper trading to funded-account trading — with enough 
consistency and capital growth to eventually replace his current $25/hr 
job income and build long-term wealth.

This is not a hobby project. This is a long-term wealth-building system 
built on process, not luck.

## What Success Looks Like
- A scanner and grading system that reliably identifies high-probability setups
- A paper trading record that proves the strategy works before real capital is risked
- A funded account workflow strict enough to pass prop firm evaluations
- Multiple funded accounts generating consistent monthly income
- Trading income funding long-term wealth building (index funds, real estate)
- Eventually: enough income to leave the $25/hr job entirely

## What We Are Building
A semi-automated options trading system focused on:
- Credit Spreads (bull put spreads on bullish setups, bear call spreads on bearish setups)
- Strict risk guardrails (3 trades/day max, 5% risk, 50% stop-loss, 30–45 DTE, 0.30 delta)
- Phone-based remote approval so trading is compatible with day job hours
- Complete audit trail of every decision, scan, and trade
- A system that tells you when you're ready to fund — not just when features are done
- Target prop firm is Black Eagle Financial Group — evaluation fee $150-500, funded accounts up to $250K, 80% profit split
- Strategy optimized for defined-risk, capital-efficient trades compatible with prop firm Greek-based risk framework

## What We Are NOT Building
- A gambling tool
- A fully autonomous system with no human oversight
- A system optimized for excitement over consistency
- Anything that risks blowing a funded account
- A system built faster than the risk process can validate it

## Long-Term Vision
1. Paper trading proves the process
2. Funded account evaluation passed with discipline
3. Multiple funded accounts running simultaneously
4. Options income supplements then replaces job income
5. Trading cash flow redirected into long-term wealth building
6. Futures explored only after options process is proven
7. Long-term wealth built through compounding, not moonshots
